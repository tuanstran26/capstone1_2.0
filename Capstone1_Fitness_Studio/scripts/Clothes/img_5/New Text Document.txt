AeroFLX+ Seamless Tee
AeroFLX+ is for when you plan to sweat a lot.

The Mens Navy AeroFLX+ Seamless Tee features body-mapped ventilation that provides air flow and breathability where you need it most. Backed by over 18 months of research and development we analysed athletes body temperatures through a range of workouts and built the fit, feel and function of the fabric based on their needs.

Ionic+™ Technology
Lightweight moisture wicking poly yarns are bonded with positively charged silver ions (that’s where the ‘+’ comes from) which neutralise body odour meaning you can go longer between washes.
